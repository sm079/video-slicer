Export windows

Video only, at the project frame rate. Every output frame is rendered exactly as the Output preview shows it.

How it works

Set the output fps (blank = native, frame for frame). Every frame on the timeline is an output frame; the viewer and the Output panel show exactly the frame that will be rendered.

Timeline

  • Drag on an empty track to draw a window; it snaps to the track's length rule.
  • Double-click an empty track, or press N, for a default-length window. Overlapping windows go to the next free track.
  • Drag a window to slide it (up/down moves it to another track), drag its edges to resize. Edges snap to the playhead and other windows; hold Alt to stop snapping.
  • Click ▶ on a window to loop it. Double-click a window to zoom to it.
  • Wheel zooms, Shift+wheel pans, right/middle-drag pans. Wheel over the track names scrolls the tracks.

Crop

  • Drag inside the box to move, handles to resize (Alt from the centre), the round handle to rotate (Shift snaps to 15°). Drag outside the box to draw a new one.
  • Turn on Animate to keyframe the crop; every edit then keys it at the playhead. Drag the ◆ marks on the timeline to retime keys.
  • Wheel zooms the viewer, right-drag pans, double-click outside the crop resets the view.
